# Spice Route Kitchens, Chennai and Bengaluru – Empanelled Caterer Profile

## §1 Overview
Spice Route Kitchens is an empanelled caterer serving events in Chennai and Bengaluru, including Whitefield Innovation Hub and other venues that permit external caterers.

## §2 Menus and Rates
Vegetarian meals cost ₹520 per head and non-vegetarian meals cost ₹690 per head. Live counters cost an extra ₹150 per head.

## §3 Minimum Order and Notice
The minimum order is 25 people, and orders must be placed at least 48 hours before the event.
