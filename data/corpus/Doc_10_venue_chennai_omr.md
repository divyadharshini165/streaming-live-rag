# OMR Tech Park Conference Centre, Sholinganallur, Chennai – Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview
OMR Tech Park Conference Centre is an approved venue in Sholinganallur, Chennai.

## §2 Rooms and Capacity
The Seminar Room seats 40 people in classroom style. The Auditorium seats 150 people in theatre style.

## §3 Pricing
The Seminar Room costs ₹20,000 for a half day and ₹34,000 for a full day. The Auditorium costs ₹55,000 for a half day and ₹90,000 for a full day.

## §4 Cancellation Policy
Cancellation is free up to 10 days before the event. Cancellations 3 to 10 days before the event incur a 50% charge, and cancellations less than 3 days before the event incur a 100% charge.

## §5 Catering
In-house vegetarian and non-vegetarian catering is available at ₹600 and ₹750 per head. External caterers are not permitted in the Auditorium.
