# Nexora Technologies – Work From Home Policy

## §1 Eligibility
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with their reporting manager's approval. Work from home is not available during the first 3 months of probation.

## §2 Core Hours
Employees working from home must be available online during core hours of 11 AM to 4 PM.

## §3 Internet Allowance
Employees on a hybrid work from home arrangement receive an internet allowance of ₹1,000 per month.
