# KP Studio, Koregaon Park, Pune – Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview
KP Studio is a small approved venue in Koregaon Park, Pune, suited to design sprints and small meetings.

## §2 Capacity
KP Studio seats a maximum of 20 people.

## §3 Pricing
KP Studio costs ₹9,000 for a half day and ₹15,000 for a full day.

## §4 Cancellation Policy
Bookings are fully refundable if cancelled more than 72 hours before the event and non-refundable within 72 hours.

## §5 Catering
Only coffee and snacks are available at KP Studio, at ₹250 per head. Meals are not served.
