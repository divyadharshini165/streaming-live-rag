# Nexora Technologies – Travel Booking Exceptions

## §1 Standard Booking Timelines
Domestic travel must be booked at least 7 days before departure and international travel at least 21 days before departure through the Travel Desk.

## §2 Late Bookings Before Travel
A booking made with less than the required notice, but before travel, is allowed with reporting manager approval and a reason entered in the Travel Desk portal. If the fare exceeds the lowest available fare by more than ₹5,000, the difference may be recovered from the employee unless the trip is marked business-critical.

## §3 Bookings Made After Travel
When tickets or hotels are booked by the employee outside the Travel Desk and claimed after travel (post-travel booking), the claim requires Senior Director approval and a written justification. Reimbursement for such bookings is capped at the Travel Desk negotiated rate for the same route and dates.

## §4 Repeated Exceptions
An employee with more than 2 booking exceptions in a quarter is reported to Finance Compliance. Any further exception in that quarter requires Vice President approval.
