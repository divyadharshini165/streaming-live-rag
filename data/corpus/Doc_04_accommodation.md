# Nexora Technologies – Accommodation Policy

## §1 Domestic Hotel Limits
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to ₹7,000 per night in metro cities and ₹4,500 per night in other cities. Employees must book Travel Desk partner hotels wherever available.

## §2 International Hotel Limits
International hotel stays are reimbursed up to USD 180 per night, or USD 250 per night in New York, London, Tokyo and Singapore.

## §3 Staying with Friends or Relatives
Employees who stay with friends, relatives or family (for example a cousin or sibling) instead of a hotel receive a flat allowance of ₹1,000 per night. No receipts are needed for this allowance.

## §4 Early Check-in and Late Check-out
Early check-in or late check-out charges are reimbursed only with prior approval from the reporting manager.
