# Nexora Technologies – Leave Policy

## §1 Annual Leave Entitlement
Each employee receives 18 days of earned leave, 12 days of casual leave and 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.

## §2 Carry Forward
Unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next year up to a maximum balance of 30 days. Casual leave and sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## §3 Applying for Leave
Planned leave must be applied for in the HR portal at least 3 days in advance. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
