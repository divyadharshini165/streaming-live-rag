# Riverside Business Centre, Baner, Pune – Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview
Riverside Business Centre is an approved venue in Baner, Pune.

## §2 Rooms and Capacity
Meeting Room A seats 25 people in classroom style. The Conference Hall seats 45 people in classroom style or 60 in theatre style.

## §3 Pricing
Meeting Room A costs ₹12,000 for a half day and ₹20,000 for a full day. The Conference Hall costs ₹25,000 for a half day and ₹42,000 for a full day. A projector is included; video conferencing equipment costs an extra ₹3,000.

## §4 Cancellation Policy
A full refund is given for cancellations made 14 or more days before the event. Cancellations made 7 to 14 days before the event incur a 25% charge. Cancellations made less than 7 days before the event incur a 100% charge.

## §5 Catering
Riverside Business Centre has no in-house kitchen. External catering from empanelled caterers is allowed with a handling fee of ₹2,000 per event.
