# Nexora Technologies – Event and Workshop Booking Policy

## §1 Scope
This policy covers internal and customer events such as workshops, trainings and team offsites.

## §2 Booking Lead Time
Events must be booked through the Events Desk at least 14 days before the event date. Events with more than 50 participants must be booked at least 30 days in advance.

## §3 Budget per Participant
The budget is ₹1,500 per participant for a half-day event and ₹2,500 per participant for a full-day event, covering venue and catering. Customer workshops may exceed this budget by up to 20% with Sales Head approval.

## §4 Approved Venues
Only venues on the Approved Venue List may be booked: Orchid Hall Pune (Doc_07), Riverside Business Centre Pune (Doc_08), KP Studio Pune (Doc_09), OMR Tech Park Conference Centre Chennai (Doc_10), Adyar Heritage Hall Chennai (Doc_11) and Whitefield Innovation Hub Bengaluru (Doc_12). Any other venue needs Admin and Procurement approval.

## §5 Catering
Catering must come from the venue's in-house kitchen or from an empanelled caterer. The empanelled caterers are Annapoorna Caterers (Doc_13) and Spice Route Kitchens (Doc_14).

## §6 Cancellation Charges
Venue and catering cancellation charges are borne by the organising department's cost centre.
