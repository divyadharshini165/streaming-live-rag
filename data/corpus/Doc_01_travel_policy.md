# Nexora Technologies – Business Travel & Reimbursement Policy

## §1 Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees travelling on company business. It covers airfare, rail, accommodation, daily allowance and local conveyance. Trips abroad and exception cases are covered by separate policies (Doc_02 and Doc_03).

## §2 Booking Process
All domestic travel must be booked through the Nexora Travel Desk portal at least 7 days before departure. Employees below Director level travel in economy class. Directors and above may book premium economy for flights longer than 5 hours.

## §3 Domestic Daily Allowance
The domestic per diem (daily allowance) is ₹2,500 per day in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Pune, Hyderabad and Kolkata) and ₹1,800 per day in all other cities. The per diem covers meals and incidentals, so separate meal receipts are not required.

## §4 Claim Submission
Reimbursement claims must be submitted in the Expense portal within 15 days of returning from the trip. Original receipts are required for any single expense above ₹500. Claims submitted more than 60 days after return are not reimbursed.

## §5 Approval Limits and Payment
The reporting manager approves claims up to ₹50,000 per trip. Claims above ₹50,000 require department head approval. Approved reimbursements are credited with the next monthly payroll.
