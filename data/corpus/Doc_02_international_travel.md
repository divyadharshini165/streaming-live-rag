# Nexora Technologies – International Travel Policy

## §1 Pre-Approval
International trips require department head approval and a travel request raised at least 21 days before departure. Visa fees and mandatory vaccination costs are reimbursed.

## §2 International Daily Allowance
The international per diem is USD 75 per day. For the USA, UK, Japan and Singapore the per diem is USD 100 per day.

## §3 Foreign Currency Receipt Verification
All expenses paid in foreign currency must be supported by original receipts and a currency conversion proof, such as a forex card statement or a bank statement showing the INR debit. Finance verifies the conversion rate against the rate on the transaction date. Receipts submitted without conversion proof are reimbursed at the RBI reference rate for that date.

## §4 Travel Insurance
Travel insurance is mandatory for all international trips and must be procured through the Travel Desk. Self-purchased insurance is not reimbursed.

## §5 International Claim Timeline
International travel claims must be submitted within 30 days of return, together with the currency conversion proof described in §3.
