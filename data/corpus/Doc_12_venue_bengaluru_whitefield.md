# Whitefield Innovation Hub, Bengaluru – Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview
Whitefield Innovation Hub is an approved venue in Whitefield, Bengaluru.

## §2 Rooms and Capacity
Studio 1 seats 30 people in classroom style. Summit Hall seats 100 people in theatre style.

## §3 Pricing
Studio 1 costs ₹16,000 for a half day and ₹27,000 for a full day. Summit Hall costs ₹45,000 for a half day and ₹75,000 for a full day.

## §4 Cancellation Policy
Cancellation is free up to 7 days before the event. Cancellations 2 to 7 days before the event incur a 50% charge, and cancellations within 48 hours incur a 100% charge.

## §5 Catering
The in-house café offers a buffet at ₹650 per head. External catering from empanelled caterers is also allowed.
