# Nexora Technologies – Training and Certification Reimbursement

## §1 Annual Limit
Employees can claim up to ₹40,000 per financial year for approved courses and certifications.

## §2 Service Commitment
Courses or certifications costing more than ₹25,000 require a 12-month service commitment after completion.

## §3 Exam Fees and Claims
Certification exam fees are reimbursed once, after the employee passes the exam. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of receiving the certificate.
