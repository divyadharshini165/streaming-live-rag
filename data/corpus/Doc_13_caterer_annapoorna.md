# Annapoorna Caterers, Pune – Empanelled Caterer Profile

## §1 Overview
Annapoorna Caterers is an empanelled caterer serving events in Pune, including venues that allow external catering such as Riverside Business Centre.

## §2 Menus and Rates
Vegetarian meals cost ₹480 per head and non-vegetarian meals cost ₹650 per head. Jain and vegan menus are available on request with 5 days' notice.

## §3 Minimum Order and Notice
The minimum order is 20 people. The final headcount must be confirmed 72 hours before the event.

## §4 Cancellation Terms
Catering orders can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the event; later cancellations are charged at 50% of the order value.
