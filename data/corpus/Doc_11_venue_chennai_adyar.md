# Adyar Heritage Hall, Chennai – Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview
Adyar Heritage Hall is an approved heritage venue in Adyar, Chennai.

## §2 Capacity
The main hall seats 50 people in theatre style or 36 in classroom style.

## §3 Pricing
The main hall costs ₹15,000 for a half day and ₹26,000 for a full day.

## §4 Cancellation Policy
A 20% cancellation fee applies to any cancellation made more than 7 days before the event. Cancellations within 7 days of the event incur a 100% charge.

## §5 Catering
Only in-house vegetarian catering (South Indian menu) is served, at ₹450 per head. A Jain menu is available with 3 days' notice.
