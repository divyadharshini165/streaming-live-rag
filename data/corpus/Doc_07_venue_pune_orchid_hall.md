# Orchid Hall, Hinjewadi, Pune – Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview
Orchid Hall is an approved venue in Hinjewadi Phase 1, Pune, about 2 km from the Nexora Pune office.

## §2 Rooms and Capacity
The Boardroom seats 12 people. The Training Room seats 35 people in classroom style or 40 in theatre style. The Grand Hall seats 60 people in classroom style or 80 in theatre style.

## §3 Pricing
The Training Room costs ₹18,000 for a half day and ₹30,000 for a full day. The Grand Hall costs ₹35,000 for a half day and ₹60,000 for a full day. Prices include a projector, two microphones and Wi-Fi.

## §4 Cancellation Policy
Cancellation is free up to 7 days before the event. Cancelling within 7 days of the event incurs a 50% charge. No refund is given for cancellations within 48 hours of the event.

## §5 Catering
Only in-house catering is allowed at Orchid Hall; outside food and external caterers are not permitted. Full-day packages with two tea breaks cost ₹550 per head (vegetarian) and ₹700 per head (non-vegetarian).
