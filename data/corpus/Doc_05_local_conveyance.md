# Nexora Technologies – Local Conveyance Policy

## §1 Cabs
App-based cabs used for business travel are reimbursed on submitting the trip receipt. Premium cab categories are not allowed unless three or more employees share the ride.

## §2 Own Vehicle Mileage
Employees using their own vehicle for business are reimbursed at ₹12 per km for a car and ₹6 per km for a two-wheeler. A trip log with date, route and distance must be attached to the mileage claim.

## §3 Airport Transfers
Cab transfers between home or hotel and the airport are reimbursed in both the origin and destination cities.
