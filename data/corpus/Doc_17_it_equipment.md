# Nexora Technologies – IT Equipment Policy

## §1 Laptop Refresh
Company laptops are refreshed every 4 years.

## §2 Lost or Stolen Devices
A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours. A copy of the police complaint (FIR) must be submitted within 7 days.

## §3 Personal Devices
Personal phones and tablets used for company email must be enrolled in the company device management (MDM) system.
